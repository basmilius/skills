# The twelve principles, on a canvas

Use exactly these names in a take's `principles` (the page's skills index counts them). Assign two or three
per take, and across a roll of twenty make sure every one appears at least once.

| Principle | What it looks like in a take |
|---|---|
| Squash and stretch | A ball or blob flattens on impact and lengthens along its velocity, volume kept. Letters squash when they slam together. A glass lens stretches in its direction of travel. |
| Anticipation | A crouch before a jump, a squeeze before letters spring apart, a key cap pressing before Enter, a tug before a plug comes out. |
| Staging | One focal point at a time: the rest dims or settles while the thing that matters moves; the camera leans toward it. |
| Straight ahead and pose to pose | A pen drawing a sketch stroke by stroke (straight ahead), then shapes morphing between held key poses (pose to pose). |
| Follow through and overlapping action | Parts arrive a few frames apart; flaps bounce after landing; cables keep swinging after the move; content lags behind its frame. |
| Slow in and slow out | Strong ease on every camera move and every transit between stations; dwell at the ends. |
| Arcs | Cards, cursors and packets travel on curves, never straight lines at constant speed. |
| Secondary action | The main action causes a smaller one: nodes bob and cables sway, a name pill trails its cursor, nodes lean when a line snaps. |
| Timing | The number of frames sells weight and meaning: a spacing chart, a pulse that travels then a beat then the wake, a clock that ticks in steps. |
| Exaggeration | Push a pose past realism so it reads at a glance: extra squash, an overshoot the eye catches. |
| Solid drawing | Believable volume and light: lit slabs, self-shadow, a 3D object rendered in glyphs. |
| Appeal | Shapes and rhythm people want to keep watching: the resolved logo, a flower of moire, a warm morning. |

Showing the craft itself is allowed and effective: onion skins, spacing charts, tick marks per frame, a
readout of tracking or frequency. It makes the CV explicit without words.
