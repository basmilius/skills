# Three proposals from a real run

Product: Ruimte (agents, terminals and browsers on an infinite canvas). Story: building the website for a
fictional festival, Nachtveld (three days, two stages, pages line-up, tickets, map). All three open on a
kinetic wordmark and end on a card with the tagline and the URL. The person asked for all three plus the
recommended blend, so four films were made.

## 1. From sketch to line-up: one continuous camera
Calm and confident, for the homepage. The build itself is the story.
0:00 wordmark, its gaps become the first nodes; 0:05 a sketch of three boxes, read in order by an agent;
0:12 a context line from sketch and brief to a chat; 0:20 the chat delegates to three agents with worktrees
and plans; 0:30 one turns amber and asks a question, answered on the canvas; 0:38 a browser node, a
screenshot, a fix; 0:46 zoom out, a merge resolved stretch by stretch; 0:54 live, pull back, end card.
Strength: one move, very legible. Weakness: views and the phone barely appear (the director solved it by
turning the canvas into one cell of a 2 x 2 grid near the end).

## 2. Rhythm: the grid as an instrument
Energetic, for social and launches. 120 BPM, every cut on a downbeat, a visible beat meter since it autoplays
muted. 0:00 wordmark on the beat; 0:04 one view, 2, 4, 9; 0:14 delegation on the beat; 0:24 a montage of one
idea per bar; 0:32 the stop: one cell turns amber, the rest dims, the beat returns only after the answer;
0:40 computer use; 0:50 phone approval; 0:56 the grid folds into the wordmark.
Strength: covers the most. Weakness: busy (fixed with two-second montage shots and one caption each).

## 3. The night before: a story with a clock
Memorable; says why the product is different: the work goes on without you. A clock in frame.
0:00 wordmark, "opens in 14 hours"; 0:05 22:00, late work, delegation; 0:15 the laptop closes, sessions keep
running on a server; 0:22 01:40 an agent hits its usage limit and resumes at the reset; 0:32 02:15 the phone
on the nightstand, answered from bed; 0:40 07:30 the laptop opens, everything where it was, a stack waits;
0:50 grid view, green, live at 11:58; 0:57 end card.
Strength: emotional, shows unique features. Weakness: less spectacle of the main surface.

## 4. Blend (recommended): the opening of 1 with the story of 3
The canvas carries the first half (sketch, context, delegation, a question), the clock story the second.
